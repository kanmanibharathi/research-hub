These statistical tools are provided free of cost to support students, researchers, and early-career scientists.

Comparable analytical software and consultation services often cost thousands of rupees or dollars, placing them out of reach for many. We believe that access to reliable scientific tools should never be a barrier to learning or research.

Maintaining secure servers, computing resources, and continuous development involves real operational costs. If this platform has helped your work, please consider a small voluntary donation to help keep these tools free, accessible, and improving for everyone.
