The Hardy–Weinberg principle describes how allele and genotype frequencies remain constant from generation to generation if no evolutionary forces act.

Basic Equations

For two alleles A (dominant) and a (recessive):
p + q = 1 (Allele Frequencies)
p² + 2pq + q² = 1 (Genotype Frequencies)

  • AA (p²): Homozygous Dominant
  • Aa (2pq): Heterozygous
  • aa (q²): Homozygous Recessive

kcat Calculation

Enter Vmax and total enzyme concentration to calculate kcat using the formula:
